OSU is more recent and more specific; UNL may reflect older taxonomy.)
- **Cephalosporium_Stripe** → `pathogen_scientific_name (teleomorph)`: canonical=`Cephalosporium gramineum`, alt=`Hymenula cerealis (sexual stage synonym)` (PNW Handbooks notes the sexual stage name as Hymenula cerealis: 'The soilborne fungus, Cephalosporium gramineum (sexual: Hymenula cerealis)'. No taxonomy validation was available to confirm current accepted name.)
- **Cephalosporium_Stripe** → `affected_parts`: canonical=`Foliar, Stem, Root, Vascular, Seed`, alt=`Foliar, Stem, Vascular (no Root or Seed listed)` (CPN and WSU list Foliar/Stem/Vascular; PNW Handbooks additionally specifies Root infection and Seed (shriveled kernels). The broader list from PNW Handbooks is used as canonical.)
- **Common_Bunt** → `pathogen_scientific_name`: canonical=`Tilletia tritici (syn. T. caries) and T. laevis (syn. T. foetida)`, alt=`Tilletia caries and T. laevis` (Crop Protection Network uses the synonym T. caries; PNW Handbooks lists T. tritici as the current name with T. caries as synonym. No taxonomy validation was provided; canonical value follows PNW Handbooks which explicitly notes the synonymy.)
- **Common_Bunt** → `affected_parts`: canonical=`Seed, Whole plant`, alt=`Seed, Foliar` (Crop Protection Network notes foliar symptoms (off-green discoloration of spikelets); PNW Handbooks emphasizes seed and whole plant. Both are included in the merged canonical value.)
- **Eyespot** → `affected_parts`: canonical=`Stem, Vascular`, alt=`Stem only` (WSU states 'Eyespot only affects stems; roots are not colonized.' OSU extension additionally identifies vascular/nutrient-flow girding.)
- **Alternaria_Blight** → `visual_symptoms.summary`: canonical=`Small, oval, chlorotic lesions irregularly scattered on leaves that enlarge into dark brown or grey sunken lesions, eventually merging and killing the entire leaf.`, alt=`Reddish brown oval spots appear on young seedlings with bright yellow margin. In severe cases, several spots coalesce to cause drying of leaves.` (eagri.org emphasises reddish-brown colour and seedling stage; Plantix describes broader disease progress across all leaves with dark brown/grey colour.)
- **Bacterial_Leaf_Streak_Black_Chaff** → `pathogen_scientific_name`: canonical=`Xanthomonas translucens`, alt=`Xanthomonas campestris pv. undulosa` (Crop Protection Network uses the older pathovar designation Xanthomonas campestris pv. undulosa; UMN Extension uses the current accepted name Xanthomonas translucens. No NCBI/MycoBank validation was available; UMN (higher-authority extension) name selected as canonical.)
- **Head_Scab** → `affected_parts`: canonical=`Seed, Stem`, alt=`Seed only` (CPN only lists Seed; OSU extension also identifies Stem as affected when fungus infects stem immediately below the head.)
- **Karnal_Bunt** → `visual_symptoms.summary`: canonical=`Blackened, sooty, fragile kernels with cracked outer layer; boat or dugout canoe shape in advanced infections; fishy odor in severe cases`, alt=`Gray to black discolored tissue limited to embryo end only but may extend over entire kernel` (PNW Handbooks emphasizes gray-to-black progression limited to embryo end; USDA APHIS describes more advanced blackened/sooty appearance with boat shape and fishy odor. Both descriptions are consistent with different disease stages.)
- **Stem_Rust** → `pathogen_scientific_name`: canonical=`Puccinia graminis f. sp. tritici`, alt=`Puccinia graminis subsp. graminis and P. graminis f. sp. tritici` (Crop Protection Network lists both subspecies graminis and f. sp. tritici; USDA ARS lists only f. sp. tritici which is the accepted form for wheat stem rust.)
- **Stripe_Rust** → `pathogen_scientific_name`: canonical=`Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici`, alt=`Puccinia striiformis` (USDA ARS lists species-level name without forma specialis; UGA extension specifies f. sp. tritici which is standard for wheat stripe rust.)
- **Powdery_Mildew** → `affected_parts`: canonical=`Foliar, Stem`, alt=`Foliar, Whole plant (any above-ground plant part)` (OSU lists leaves, stems, and heads; UGA states the disease may occur on any above-ground plant part but is most prevalent on upper surface of lower leaves.)
- **Wheat_Leaf_Rust** → `visual_symptoms.summary`: canonical=`Brown, generally circular uredinia appearing on the upper leaf surface (USDA Cereal Disease Lab)`, alt=`Orange to brown pustules scattered randomly on leaf surface, round to slightly elongated, medium-sized (Crop Protection Network)` (USDA government source used as canonical; CPN extension description is complementary and consistent in describing scattered, non-striped pustules)
- **Tan_Spot** → `affected_parts`: canonical=`Foliar, Stem, Seed`, alt=`Foliar, Seed (no stem mentioned)` (UNL CropWatch notes the fungus invades straw at plant maturity; CPN does not mention stem involvement explicitly. Merged to include all affected parts mentioned across sources.)
